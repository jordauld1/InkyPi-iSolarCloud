Optional. Leave blank to use your first power station. To choose another, refresh once with it blank, then run journalctl -u inkypi -n 100 on the Pi: the log lists every station's ID and name.
Credentials: Add the following keys to your .env file:
ISOLARCLOUD_USERNAME – iSolarCloud account username
ISOLARCLOUD_PASSWORD – iSolarCloud account password
ISOLARCLOUD_APPKEY – Developer Portal app key
ISOLARCLOUD_SECRET – Developer Portal secret key
